STRIDE

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Your walks

Your walks never leave your phone.

Your walks with their routes, steps and photos, the routes you plan and your profile are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded: only what is described below leaves the phone, and a picture only when you share it. Deleting Stride deletes them.

Street maps

To plan routes, Stride downloads the streets around you from its map server (50-114-185-14.sslip.io). The server sees which areas were asked for (squares of about 10 to 20 km, and an index of the wider region) and your IP address, never your walks or routes, and keeps no logs. Now and then Stride also asks it for the date of its newest data, which sends nothing about your area.

The place names that come with the streets stay on your phone. Stride uses them to name each downloaded area; nothing is sent for that.

Weather

For the weather of a walk, Stride asks Open-Meteo for the weather of your area or district: the position of the nearest named district or town (from the place names on your phone), or, with none near, the walk's start rounded to about 10 km; and the walk's date. Never your exact position or your route.

The map

The map you see is Apple Maps, drawn by iOS; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

No tracking

No account, no tracking, no analytics and no ads. Stride does not collect anything about how you use it, and the developer receives no data about you or your walks.

Permissions

Location “While Using” records the walks you start, also with the screen locked. “Always” is asked during setup and when you turn on automatic walk detection, which watches for walks while Stride is closed. Motion & Fitness counts steps and climbs; notifications tell you about breaks and saved walks. Photos: Stride shows only the photos taken during a walk, and saves pictures only when you share one.

Children

Stride collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
