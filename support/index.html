Help

A walk stops recording when the phone is locked

Allow location “While Using” with Precise Location on (iPhone Settings › Stride › Location), and do not force-quit Stride during a walk.

Steps, cadence or climbs are missing

Turn on Motion & Fitness for Stride in iPhone Settings › Stride.

Walk detection does not start

It needs location “Always”, Motion & Fitness and notifications. Turning it on in Stride asks for them.

Routes cannot be planned here

Stride needs the internet once to download the streets around you. After that, routes are planned on the phone.

Where are my walks stored?

Only on your phone; they are never uploaded. Deleting Stride deletes them.
